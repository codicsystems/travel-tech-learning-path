The three written modules are the three that matter most if you are starting a
travel project this week. The rest are being added as time allows, and
contributions from people working in travel are very welcome — particularly on
regional differences outside the UK and Gulf.

Each module follows the same shape: what the concept is, why it matters
commercially, the specific mistakes engineers make, and a short list of things to
go and find out about a real operator.
